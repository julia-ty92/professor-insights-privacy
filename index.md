# Privacy Policy for Professor Insights

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Professor Insights helps students view public professor-rating information while researching and selecting courses.

## Information Professor Insights Uses

When you use Professor Insights on a supported course page or search for a professor, the extension may use course-related information displayed in the browser, such as an instructor name, course information, or school affiliation. This information is used only to locate and display relevant public professor-rating information.

Professor Insights may store user-selected school settings and saved professors locally in the user’s browser. This information is not used for advertising or sold to any third party.

## Third-Party Services

Professor Insights uses the PolyRatings API to retrieve public professor-rating information. When necessary to provide results, the extension may send the minimum information needed to match a professor or course, such as a professor name, school, or course-related search term.

Professor Insights does not collect or share passwords, payment information, contact information, student identification numbers, or account credentials.

## How Information Is Used

Information used by Professor Insights is used only to:

- Match instructors or courses with public professor-rating information
- Display ratings and related information to the user
- Store saved-professor and school-preference settings locally in the browser

Professor Insights does not sell user data, use user data for advertising, or use information for purposes unrelated to providing the extension’s features.

## Data Security

Professor Insights limits information use to what is necessary to provide its professor-insight and course-selection features. Users should avoid entering sensitive personal information into the extension.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if Professor Insights changes how it handles information. Any updates will be posted on this page.
